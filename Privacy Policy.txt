Privacy Policy for CryptoDept
Last Updated: May 11, 2026

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how CryptoDept ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information. We value your privacy and aim to be transparent about our data practices.

2. Data Collection

Personal Data: CryptoDept does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Financial Data: The app does not access your bank accounts, credit card information, or crypto wallets. It is a tracking tool, not an exchange or a wallet.

Usage Data: We do not track your search history or the specific assets you monitor. All watchlist data is stored locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services
The app fetches market data from public cryptocurrency APIs. These third-party services may receive your IP address as part of the standard technical process of making an HTTPS request, but no other identifying data is shared with them.

4. Data Security
Since all your settings and watchlists are stored locally, your data remains under your control. We use industry-standard HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the app and the market data providers.

5. Children’s Privacy
Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the contact information provided in the Google Play Store listing.
