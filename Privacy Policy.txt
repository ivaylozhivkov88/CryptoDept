### 1. MISSION STATEMENT
At CryptoDept, we operate on a "Zero-Knowledge" philosophy. Our objective is to provide institutional-grade intelligence without compromising the operator's digital footprint.

*   **Encryption:** Secured via SQLCipher AES-256 protocol.
*   **Access:** We have NO remote access to your local database. If you delete the app, the data enclave is purged.

*   [ ] Personally Identifiable Information (Name, Email, Phone).

*   **AI Link:** Narrative reports are generated via Google Gemini. No personal data is sent to the AI engine — only raw market numbers.

### 5. SYSTEM STABILITY MONITORING

*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** Anonymous logs of system failures.
*   **Opt-out:** You can deactivate stability monitoring at any time in [Settings > Security].

### 6. COMPLIANCE & DELETION
You are the master of your data.
*   **Manual Purge:** Use the "Wipe All Data" command in Settings to reset the terminal.
