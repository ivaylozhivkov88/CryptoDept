# Privacy Policy
**Effective Date:** May 22, 2026

At **CryptoDept**, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This policy outlines how we handle information within the CryptoDept Terminal Android application.

## 1. Data Collection
We minimize data collection to the absolute necessity for terminal functionality:
*   **Portfolio Data:** Any coin watchlist or portfolio data you input is stored **locally on your device** in an encrypted database unless you choose to sync with our cloud backup.
*   **Device Info:** We collect anonymous hardware identifiers for licensing and anti-fraud purposes (Google Play Billing).
*   **Usage Logs:** Anonymous crash reports and performance metrics are collected via Firebase Crashlytics.

## 2. API Keys
If you provide personal API keys for external services (e.g., Etherscan), these keys are stored in the **Android Keystore System** and never transmitted to our servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We integrate with the following providers:
*   **CoinGecko / CoinGlass:** For market data.
*   **Firebase (Google):** For authentication, cloud-sync, and notifications.
*   **Google Play Billing:** For Pro-tier subscriptions.

## 4. Cloud Sync (Optional)
If you enable Cloud Sync, your watchlist and terminal settings are stored on our secure Firebase servers. This data is encrypted and linked only to your anonymous UID.
