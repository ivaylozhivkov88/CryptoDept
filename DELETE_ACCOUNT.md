# How to Delete Your CryptoDept Account

We believe you should have full control over your data.

## Step 1: Request Deletion via App
1. Open the **CryptoDept Terminal**.
2. Navigate to **Settings** (⚙️).
3. Select **Profile & Security**.
4. Scroll to the bottom and tap **[ DELETE_ACCOUNT ]**.
5. Confirm your identity via biometric or password.

## Step 2: Request Deletion via Email
If you no longer have access to the app, please email us from the account used to register:
*   **Email:** `admin@cryptodept.io`
*   **Subject:** Account Deletion Request - [Your Name/ID]

## Data Removal Policy
Upon request:
*   All cloud-synced watchlists will be purged.
*   Your anonymous Firebase UID will be deleted.
*   Local data on your device will remain but can no longer be synced. (You must clear app cache manually to remove local device data).
